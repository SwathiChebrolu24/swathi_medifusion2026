# Uploads

## Patient X-ray upload

The patient dashboard accepts one image per X-ray case:

- Formats: JPEG/JPG, PNG, or WebP
- Maximum size: 10 MB
- The image should be a clear chest X-ray or other diagnostic scan
- The image must not be empty

The upload is associated with the logged-in patient account. Patient name and contact details are taken from the authenticated account; they do not need to be entered for an X-ray upload.

## Prescription AI

Prescription and medicine images can be uploaded from `ai_prescription.html`. Use a clear JPEG, PNG, or WebP image with the medicine name, label, or prescription text visible.

## Lab report summary

The lab report analyzer currently accepts report text pasted into the text area. It does not upload PDF or document files.

AI results are preliminary and must be reviewed by a qualified clinician.
